## Project concept

The aim is to create a more intuitive tool for managing living expenses: enter a monthly allowance and planned savings, then set a budget around meals, entertainment, and travel plans.

## Planned modules

- Monthly budgets and savings goals
- Spending records by category
- Remaining budget and forecasts of future spending
- Bill statistics and reminders

Features such as automatically recognizing spending records still require further evaluation of Android system permissions, privacy, and payment channel restrictions. This page records a concept only; it does not mean these features have been implemented.
