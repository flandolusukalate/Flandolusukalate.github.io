## A few unfinished ideas

I like keeping a record of the things I am trying. They may not have become mature projects yet, but the process itself is often worth documenting.

It might be a small piece of software, a study note, a new UI color palette, or an everyday tool that suddenly comes to mind.

## It is okay to take it slowly

If every page had to be "finished" before it could go online, many interesting parts of the process would stay hidden.

Unfinished notes have a place here. Technical articles can be updated, project statuses can change, and past opinions can be revised through new experience.

May this place feel like a study with a window: a room that can always be tidied up, with space for more books. ✧
