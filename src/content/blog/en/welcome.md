## Hello, and welcome

If the internet were a vast sea of stars, this little site would be a light of my own.

Over time, it will become home to coding notes, software development experiments, project stories, and small moments from life worth keeping.

## Why build this website?

I hope it can be more than a portfolio page: a knowledge garden that keeps growing.

- **Record**: Write down ideas from learning and development.
- **Organize**: Gradually turn scattered knowledge into searchable articles.
- **Showcase**: Give projects complete descriptions and a record of their progress.
- **Share**: Exchange inspiration with friends who happen to stop by.

## About this digital study

The site is built with Astro and published on GitHub Pages. Technical content is written in Markdown, and the pages aim to stay lightweight and clear.

The book pages, star trails, and dragon girl in the background bring a touch of fantasy to this little site. What matters most is still the writing kept here.

I look forward to opening this post again someday and seeing how far I have come. ✦
